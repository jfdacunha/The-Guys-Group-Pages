Why It Matters

What Happens When a Ducted System Goes Uncleaned

Mould and Bacteria Through Every Vent

A ducted system pushes air through every room in your home. When mould builds up in the unit, every vent spreads it. Regular cleaning eliminates it at the source.

Power Bills Keep Climbing

A blocked filter and dirty coil can reduce efficiency by up to 30%. A clean system cools your whole home faster and runs for less. The clean pays for itself.

Expensive Breakdowns

Dust and debris accelerate wear on compressors, fans and motors. Annual professional cleaning is the most cost-effective way to extend the life of your system.
